# UNILOG — Student User Guide

As an enrolled student (e.g. `Aarav Patel`, Roll No `CSE001`), UNILOG gives you clear, real-time insight into your lecture attendance, academic calendar, exam timetable, internal marks, and correction appeals.

---

## Task 1: Monitoring Your Overall Attendance Status
1. Log in and navigate to `/student` (Student Dashboard).
2. Look at the large **Attendance Ring**:
   - **Green (≥75%)**: You are in the safe zone and fully compliant with university eligibility guidelines.
   - **Amber (60%–74%)**: Moderate risk; caution advised.
   - **Red (<60%)**: Critical risk; immediate action required.
3. If your attendance falls below 75%, an **Attendance Warning Banner** appears prominently at the top calculating the exact number of consecutive lectures you must attend to restore your safe standing.
4. Review your **Subject Cards**:
   - Each card displays your attendance percentage, classes attended out of total held, and a neumorphic progress bar.

---

## Task 2: Reviewing Detailed Subject-Wise Attendance
1. In the sidebar, navigate to **My Attendance** (`/student/attendance`).
2. Filter by specific subject or view all registered courses.
3. Inspect detailed metrics:
   - Subject Code & Course Title
   - Number of lectures attended vs total sessions held
   - Current percentage
   - Standing status (`SAFE` vs `AT_RISK`)
   - Number of additional lectures needed to hit the mandatory 75% cutoff.

---

## Task 3: Inspecting Your Monthly Attendance Calendar
1. Navigate to **Calendar** (`/student/calendar`).
2. Use the **Previous Month** and **Next Month** arrows to navigate terms.
3. Every day in the calendar reflects your attendance status via intuitive color coding:
   - **Green**: Attended all scheduled lectures.
   - **Amber**: Partial attendance or late arrival.
   - **Red**: Recorded absent for all classes that day.
   - **Indigo Dashed**: Official college holiday.
   - **Gray**: No classes scheduled.
4. Click on any date cell to view lecture times, course codes, and exact roll-call records for that day in the side panel.

---

## Task 4: Submitting an Attendance Correction Request
If you were marked absent mistakenly (e.g., while representing the college at an authorized event or on approved medical leave):
1. Navigate to **Requests** (`/student/requests`).
2. Click **New Correction Request**.
3. Select the disputed session from the dropdown (automatically lists sessions where you were marked absent or late).
4. Enter a detailed justification explaining the error.
5. Upload a supporting PDF or image document (e.g. permission letter or medical certificate).
6. Click **Submit Appeal**.
7. Track your appeal status on the visual timeline: `Submitted` → `Under Review` → `Approved` or `Rejected`. Once approved by faculty or administration, your attendance percentage updates automatically.

---

## Task 5: Viewing CIE Internal Exam Marks
1. Navigate to **CIE Marks** (`/student/cie`).
2. Review the interactive performance bar chart comparing your results across courses.
3. Inspect the breakdown table showing your scores and percentages for `CIE-1` and `CIE-2`.
